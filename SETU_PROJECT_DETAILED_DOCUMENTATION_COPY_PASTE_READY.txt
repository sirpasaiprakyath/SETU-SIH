1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROBLEM CONTEXT
====================================================================================================

1.1 The Frontline Reality:
The Central Armed Police Forces (CRPF, BSF, CISF, ITBP, SSB, NSG, Assam Rifles)—with the Central 
Reserve Police Force (CRPF) serving as the lead internal security force of the Union—operate under 
the most demanding operational crucibles:
• High-intensity Counter-Insurgency & Counter-Terrorist operations in Jammu & Kashmir.
• Dense jungle warfare and continuous anti-Maoist patrols across the Left-Wing Extremism (LWE) heartland 
  (Bastar, Sukma, Dantewada, Gadchiroli).
• Severe climatological extremes ranging from -30°C in high-altitude Himalayan forward posts to 
  +50°C in the Thar Desert and hyper-humid tropical forests of the North-East.
• Relentless operational demands including perimeter defense, election deployments, VIP security, 
  and vital installation security with fragmented circadian rest.

1.2 The Silent Crisis (Non-Combat Casualties):
Official data presented to Parliament and the Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) demonstrates that 
suicide, severe burnout, and welfare-related attrition remain urgent institutional priorities:
• The Department-Related Parliamentary Standing Committee on Home Affairs (242nd Report, 2023) 
  recorded 654 CAPF personnel suicides between 2018 and 2022.
• Subsequent official MHA parliamentary disclosures record sustained annual losses across CAPFs, 
  NSG, and Assam Rifles: 158 in 2021, 138 in 2022, 157 in 2023, 149 in 2024, and 143 in 2025.
• Non-combat attrition from chronic sleep disruption, prolonged family separation, domestic legal 
  disputes, and operational fatigue consistently rivals hostile combat casualties.

1.3 Why Conventional Civilian Mental Health Solutions Fail in Uniformed Services:
1. The Stigma and Career Fear Barrier:
   In armed and paramilitary forces, admitting psychological distress or depression on a generic 
   wellness app is perceived as a career-ending move. Soldiers fear being stigmatized by peers, 
   downgraded in medical category (SHAPE-1 status loss), de-authorized from carrying service weapons, 
   passed over for promotion, or marked negatively in Annual Confidential Reports (ACR).
2. The "Cross-Personnel Average" Statistical Flaw:
   Civilian tools evaluate individuals by comparing questionnaire scores against static, population-wide 
   group averages. An introverted commando who speaks minimally by nature is erroneously flagged as 
   "withdrawn", while an extrovert whose communication drops by 80% remains undetected because their 
   absolute score stays above the group norm.
3. Surveillance vs. Healing Paranoia:
   When soldiers know administrative commanders can inspect self-care logs, breathing sessions, or 
   stress scores, they boycott the system or enter false positive responses.
4. Austere Network Realities:
   Tactical Forward Operating Bases (FOBs) in remote jungles and border outposts frequently experience 
   power cuts and operate with zero internet or intermittent 2G/satellite links (PolNet). Cloud-dependent 
   SaaS solutions fail immediately.

2. PROPOSED SOLUTION: SETU (GUARDIAN MINDS)
====================================================================================================

2.1 Solution Overview:
SETU ("The Bridge") is an institutional, multi-tiered early-warning psychological and operational 
welfare triage platform architected specifically to overcome the structural, cultural, and 
infrastructural barriers of the Indian Armed Forces.

SETU does not replace clinical doctors, psychiatrists, or unit commanding officers. Instead, it acts 
as an operational decision-support bridge—identifying subtle, cumulative shifts in operational exhaustion, 
sleep fragmentation, and duty strain weeks before they escalate into acute psychiatric crises, 
self-harm, or disciplinary breakdown.

2.2 Core Architectural Innovations & Differentiators:

1. Personal Baseline Deviation Normalization (Moving Z-Score Engine):
   SETU scraps static cross-soldier population averages. Each personnel is evaluated solely against their 
   own historical 90-day rolling baseline across six operational vectors (sleep continuity, duty hours, 
   social engagement, physical stamina, family distress, and peer friction). A flag is raised only 
   when an individual deviates significantly from their personal equilibrium (ΔZ = [X_t - μ_i] / σ_i).

2. Dual-Track Privacy Architecture (Vishram vs. Saathi):
   • Track 1: "Vishram" (विश्राम) — The 100% Unmonitored Sanctuary:
     A dedicated mental recovery and self-care space featuring tactical box-breathing animations, 
     grounding exercises, and pre-cached Indian classical flute (Bansuri) audio. Crucially, Vishram is 
     telemetry-free and analytics-isolated: it sends zero logs, creates zero database rows, computes 
     zero scores, and provides zero commander visibility. Soldiers recharge without fear of observation.
   • Track 2: "Saathi" (साथी) — The 60-Second Micro Check-In:
     A non-intrusive, rapid operational check-in accessible in bilingual Hindi and English. It uses 
     tactical sliders to assess objective metrics: sleep duration/quality, duty shift overextension, 
     physical fatigue, and family peace using military-neutral vocabulary.

3. "Dhvani" Zero-Trust Acoustic Signal Processing Engine:
   • Client-side Web Audio API extracts vocal perturbation features (Fundamental Frequency, Jitter, 
     Shimmer, Harmonics-to-Noise Ratio) in under 15 seconds directly in browser volatile RAM.
   • 100% Privacy & DPDP Act Compliant: Float32Array PCM audio buffers are processed in volatile RAM 
     and immediately overwritten (<50ms). Zero voice audio is ever recorded, stored to disk, or 
     transmitted over the network.

4. Triangulated Signal Intelligence (Multi-Source Verification):
   SETU triangulates four distinct operational channels to prevent false positives and masking:
   a. Frontline Personnel Self-Report (60-second Saathi Check-In).
   b. Section Muster Log (NCO / Havildar bi-daily environmental report).
   c. Anonymous Buddy Drop Box (Peer observations for meal skipping or social withdrawal).
   d. Administrative Friction Telemetry (Leave delays, family court litigation, pension/SPARSH grievances).

5. Non-Intrusive Bi-Daily Section Muster (48h–72h Cadence):
   To prevent survey fatigue, Section Commanders (Havildars/NCOs) log section-level environmental loads 
   (e.g., night ambush duty, extreme cold, delayed rations) every 2 to 3 days in under 90 seconds. This 
   provides platoon context that separates individual psychological distress from unit-wide fatigue.

6. Explainable AI (XAI) with Calibrated Likelihood Dossiers:
   Instead of outputting an opaque black-box risk label, SETU utilizes a calibrated XGBoost classifier 
   coupled with TreeSHAP. Unit Medical Officers receive exact, transparent feature attributions 
   (e.g., Sleep disruption: +42%, Consecutive night sentry shifts: +31%, Peer alert: +18%, Leave denial: +9%).

7. Complete Institutional Segregation (Strict 5-Tier RBAC):
   Psychological dossiers and clinical doctor notes are cryptographically sealed from operational 
   commanding officers. Line commanders receive only anonymized, aggregate unit health indexes to 
   manage rest rotations, completely neutralizing the fear of disciplinary retaliation.

3.2 Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Architecture:
The platform enforces five strictly segregated operational roles:

1. Personnel Portal (Constables / Head Constables / Frontline Jawan):
   • Bilingual (Hindi / English) toggle available on every screen.
   • 60-second Saathi micro check-in with non-pathologizing operational sliders.
   • Telemetry-free, analytics-isolated Vishram recovery space (tactical breathing, Bansuri audio).
   • Personal leave balance tracker and SPARSH defense pension grievance helpdesk.
   • Boundary: Strict data isolation; zero access to peer records or battalion data.

2. NCO / Section Commander Portal (Havildars / Platoon Sergeants):
   • Bi-daily Section Muster (48h–72h frequency): logs patrol hours, terrain difficulty, sleep cycles.
   • Anonymous Buddy Drop Box: allows troops to flag comrades showing non-verbal warning signs 
     (skipping meals, uncharacteristic withdrawal, giving away possessions) with absolute anonymity.
   • Boundary: Cannot view private Saathi responses or clinical medical records.

3. Medical Officer Portal (Unit Doctor / Psychiatrist):
   • High-Priority Clinical Triage Queue prioritized by calibrated distress likelihood.
   • Detailed Soldier Dossier displaying individual Z-score trajectories and TreeSHAP attribution bars.
   • Confidential Clinical Notes desk: legally protected from battalion operational command.
   • Tele-MANAS & Base Hospital referral coordination workflow.

4. Welfare Officer Portal (2IC / Battalion Welfare Office):
   • Battalion-wide welfare tracking and NCO section muster oversight.
   • Family Support Grievance Desk (land dispute liaison with civil district magistrates, children 
     education allowances, medical insurance aid).
   • Resettlement / DGR portal for retiring or medically downgraded personnel.

5. System Admin & HQ Portal (Battalion Systems Officer):
   • System telemetry and node status (Edge offline sync monitoring).
   • Dual IST / UTC Immutable Audit Ledger: tracks every authentication, profile view, and triage action.
   • High-contrast accessibility and GIGW 3.0 governance compliance monitoring.
   • Boundary: Purely administrative; zero access to psychological check-ins or doctor notes.

3.3 Data Flow & Machine Learning Pipeline:
The four-stage processing pipeline executes in under 45ms per record:

Stage 1: Multi-Channel Signal Ingestion
• Personnel 60s Saathi Check-In (Sleep, Fatigue, Family Connection)
• NCO Section Muster (Patrol shifts, Night sentry hours, Extreme weather, Rations)
• Anonymous Buddy Box Alert (Peer observations for uncharacteristic withdrawal)
• Administrative Database (Leave request status, Family court cases, SPARSH grievances)

Stage 2: Personal Baseline Z-Score Normalization
• Formula: ΔZ_{i,k,t} = (X_{i,k,t} - μ_{i,k,90d}) / σ_{i,k,90d}
• Section Cohort Contextual Filter: Evaluates ΔZ against platoon averages. If all members of a 
  section experience sleep drops after an ambush, the system flags unit combat fatigue rather 
  than misclassifying individuals with psychiatric pathology.

Stage 3: Calibrated Gradient-Boosted Classification & Explainability
• Normalized vectors are ingested by the trained XGBoost model.
• Platt Sigmoidal Scaling transforms the raw margin into a calibrated posterior probability.
• TreeSHAP computes exact local Shapley feature attributions (φ_j) in real time (<15ms).

Stage 4: Confidential Role-Segregated Triage Dispatch
• High Calibrated Likelihood: Dispatches confidential alert exclusively to Unit Medical Officer.
• Dominant Family / Administrative Friction: Generates informal welfare ping to Welfare Officer.
• Line Commandants: Receive only aggregate, anonymized readiness indices (e.g., "Bravo Company: 
  Fatigue elevated; rest rotation recommended") with zero soldier names or private medical data.

3.4 Tactical Edge & Offline-First Resilience:
• Deployable on standard Commercial-Off-The-Shelf (COTS) hardware (Intel i3 laptops, Android tablets, 
  Raspberry Pi 4 edge servers).
• In remote FOBs/COBs without internet, the system runs locally on SQLite with WAL mode.
• Micro check-ins are encrypted locally using AES-256-GCM.
• When satellite or cellular (PolNet, CAPF-WAN, or 2G) connectivity is restored, an asynchronous 
  differential sync daemon transmits encrypted batches to the Battalion Core Server.
